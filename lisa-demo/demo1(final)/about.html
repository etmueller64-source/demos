About the studio

Trusted with your perfect day

A boutique full-planning and design studio, led with class, sophistication and care.

“Class, sophistication and caring are the qualities you will find when you place your trust, and your event, in the capable hands of LKE.”

Lisa K Events specializes in making your dream wedding or special event come to life — no matter how big or small. We understand that every event is different, that's why LKE makes sure to work with you each step of the way to craft your perfect day.
