vi. Questions, answered

Before you ask

Do you only plan weddings?

Not at all. Alongside full-service wedding planning, we design and execute birthdays, anniversaries, galas, engagements, proposals, bridal and baby showers, launch events and more.

What does full planning include?

Everything from start to finish — assistance with every detail pertaining to your special day, with us beside you each step of the way so you feel completely at ease.

Do you take on intimate events?

Yes — no matter how big or small. Whether you're looking for something small and intimate or large and lavish, we'll craft an event you'll never forget.

How do we start?

Reach out through the contact page with your date, guest count and vision. We'll set up a conversation from there — ready to start planning something special? So are we.
