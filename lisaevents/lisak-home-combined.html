Meet Lisa

I watch the details so you can enjoy the day

Hi, I'm Lisa. I may be small but I've got a whole lot of energy and passion, especially when it comes to people and helping them plan their perfect event. I've been planning for several years — weddings, corporate functions, fundraising galas and everything in between.

How it works

Four steps, in order

Planning with me follows the same shape every time, so you always know what's next and what's needed from you.

  1. First

    Consultation

    A relaxed call about your date, venue, guest count and budget. Free, no pressure.

  2. Then

    Design

    A concept, palette and layout built around your vision, with a vendor shortlist and full budget.

  3. Next

    Coordination

    I handle bookings, contracts, payments and the timeline. You approve; I chase.

  4. Finally

    Wedding day

    I'm on site from setup to last dance so the only thing on your list is being present.
