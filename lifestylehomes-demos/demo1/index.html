About Us

A different kind of home improvement company

For 20 years, families across York Region have trusted Lifestyles Home Creations to turn unfinished basements, dated kitchens, and tired bathrooms into spaces they actually love coming home to. We're still a small, hands-on team — which means the person who quotes your project is the same person who stands behind it when the work is done.

We believe quality lives in the details: the drywall seam you'll never see, the tile line that's perfectly level, the cleanup at the end of every single day. It's why so many of our clients say the same thing when we're finished — that our crew started to feel like part of the family.

"Your Comfort is Our Happiness." — that's been our motto since day one, and it still is.
