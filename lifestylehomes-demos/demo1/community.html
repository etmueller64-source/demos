Supporting Our Community

Twenty years in business has taught us that a home renovation company can do more than renovate homes.

Giving Back

It's part of who we are, not a side project

Our team regularly supports local organizations that assist people in need, donating building materials, tools, and our own time and services. For more than two decades, every Christmas Day, we've packed and delivered gift bags — food, clothing, blankets, hats, socks, gloves — to homeless individuals and shelters across Toronto.

It's a tradition that started small and just never stopped. We've been thanked by organizations like the Salvation Army for showing up, year after year, without being asked twice.

Local Sports

Proud Sponsor of AYSC & the Aurora Jays

We're a proud sponsor of the Aurora Youth Soccer Club and the Aurora Jays. There's nothing better than watching the next generation develop teamwork skills, get some exercise, and just have fun playing the game.

Global Giving

Helping Communities Overseas

Beyond our own backyard, we've sent full containers of clothing, tools, and food to support communities in poverty-stricken areas around the world — because a home improvement company with the means to help has a reason to.

Hurricane Sandy Relief

Rebuilding homes in Staten Island

In October 2012, Hurricane Sandy devastated communities across Staten Island. One member of our team spent two full months on the ground helping rebuild homes for families who had lost almost everything. These are their stories, in their own words.
