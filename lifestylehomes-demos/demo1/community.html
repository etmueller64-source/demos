Supporting Our Community

Twenty years in business has taught us that a home renovation company can do more than renovate homes.

Giving Back

It's part of who we are, not a side project

Our team regularly supports local organizations that assist people in need, donating building materials, tools, and our own time and services. For more than two decades, every Christmas Day, we've packed and delivered gift bags — food, clothing, blankets, hats, socks, gloves — to homeless individuals and shelters across Toronto.

It's a tradition that started small and just never stopped. We've been thanked by organizations like the Salvation Army for showing up, year after year, without being asked twice.
