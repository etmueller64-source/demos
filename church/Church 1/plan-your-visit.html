Coming with kids?

Check in at the Kids' Church table in the foyer and we'll get your child a name tag and matching pick-up tag. They'll stay with you for the opening songs, then head out for stories, crafts, and games.

Every volunteer is police-checked and trained. If your little one would rather stay beside you, that's genuinely fine — nobody here minds a bit of noise.
