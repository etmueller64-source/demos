Who we are

Small enough to know your name

We're not a big production — we're a small congregation that's called downtown Newmarket home for years, and we like it that way. It means you're not a face in a crowd here; you're someone we're genuinely glad to see.

Come with questions, come with doubts, come with none of the above. Whoever you are when you walk in is exactly who's welcome.
