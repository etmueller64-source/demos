Kids & Families

A safe, energetic place for kids

Our kids' program runs alongside the Sunday service, so grown-ups get to actually be present for worship while kids get age-appropriate teaching, games, and a whole lot of energy burned off.

  • Runs every Sunday during the 10:00 AM service
  • Age groups from nursery through grade 6
  • Family events through the year — potlucks, seasonal gatherings

Community Groups

Smaller circles, through the week

Sunday mornings are only part of it — our community groups meet through the week in homes and around the neighbourhood for conversation, prayer, and honestly, just good company.

  • Groups meet weekly or biweekly, evenings and daytime options
  • Open to newcomers any time of year, no commitment required
  • New groups form each season — ask what's starting up

Outreach

Showing up for our neighbours

Faith that stays inside these walls isn't much use to anyone. Our outreach efforts are hands-on and practical — the kind of help that actually makes a difference in downtown Newmarket.

  • Seasonal food drives & community cleanups
  • Partnerships with local shelters & support organizations
  • One-off volunteer opportunities posted through the year
