Who we are

A community first, a building second

We're a small, close-knit congregation that's been part of downtown Newmarket for years — not because we have all the answers, but because we've found that faith is easier to carry together than alone.

Whether you grew up in church, walked away from one, or have never set foot in one before, you'll find people here who are glad you came, no strings attached.
