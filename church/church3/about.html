About Us

A community first, a building second

We're not a perfect church — just a group of people trying to figure out faith together, and glad to have you along for it.

Our story

Small, and we like it that way

We're a small, close-knit congregation that's called downtown Newmarket home for years — not because we have all the answers, but because we've found that faith is easier to carry together than alone.

We're part of the Church of the Nazarene, a tradition that puts as much weight on how we treat our neighbours as on what we believe. In practice, that means you'll find us doing as much outside these walls as inside them.

Whether you grew up in church, walked away from one, or have never set foot in one before, you'll find people here who are glad you came — no strings attached, no pressure to have it all figured out.
