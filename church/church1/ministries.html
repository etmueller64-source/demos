Ministries & groups

Simple ways to be part of things.

No sign-up sheets, no commitment required. Turn up once and see how it feels.

Sundays · 10:00 AM

Sunday Worship

Familiar hymns alongside newer songs, a message rooted in scripture, and time to pray together. Nothing rushed, nothing showy.

Wednesdays · 10:00 AM

Wednesday Fellowship

The kettle is always on. A warm mid-week table for anyone who'd like company, conversation, and a listening ear.

Fridays · 7:00 PM

Friday Men's Group

Men of every age gather to talk plainly about faith, family, and the week that's been. New faces are genuinely welcome.

Sundays during the service

Kids' Church

Ages 3 to 11 head out after the opening songs for stories, crafts, and games with screened, caring volunteers.

2nd & 4th Fridays · 7:00 PM

Youth Nights

Grades 6 to 12. Pizza, games, and real conversations about the things that matter, led by adults who show up consistently.

As needed, year round

Care & Meals Team

Rides to appointments, meals after a hospital stay, a phone call on a hard week. Ask, or offer — both are welcome.

Not sure where you'd fit?

Most people start on a Sunday morning and let the rest sort itself out. If you'd rather ask a question first, we'd love to hear from you.
