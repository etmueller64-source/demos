Give

Give

Give if and when it's right for you.

Giving keeps the lights on, the coffee brewing, and the Care & Meals Team running — but it is never the price of admission here. Visitors are never asked to give.

Ways to give

However's easiest for you.

In the offering plate

Passed during Sunday Worship. Cash, cheque, or a blank envelope if you'd rather give quietly.

E-transfer

Send to give@newmarketnazarene.ca — no password required, it deposits automatically.

In person or by mail

Drop by the church office (Tue–Thu, 9:00 AM–3:00 PM) or mail a cheque to 79 Main St S, Newmarket, ON L3Y 3Y7.

Where it goes

Building upkeep, the Care & Meals Team, Kids' Church supplies, and a standing commitment to missions beyond Newmarket. Ask us anytime for specifics — we're glad to talk about it.
