A church that's been part of this town for over a century — and still saves a spot for whoever walks in next.

Radical welcome

No dress code, no quiz at the door, no assumptions about where you've been. You belong here before you believe anything.

Every generation

Ninety-year-olds and newborns in the same room. It's noisy sometimes, and we wouldn't have it any other way.

Rooted in Newmarket

Meals, clean-ups, rides, and quiet help for our neighbours — the same block, week after week, since 1908.
