Getting started

How tryouts work at AKBA

1

Register first

Every player registers with AKBA before tryouts — this is how we know your child's age group and how to reach you with dates.

2

Find your program

House League is open to everyone, no tryout needed. Rep and HP are competitive travel programs and are tryout-based.

3

Attend your tryout

Rep and HP tryouts are grouped by age. Check the schedule below, or watch your email — coaches also reach out directly.

4

Team placement

After tryouts, the coaching staff places players on rosters and reaches out with next steps, schedules, and equipment info.

Which program is my child?

Not every program needs a tryout

No tryout needed

House League

Our recreational program is open to every player who registers, from Rally Cap (ages 4–5) through 18U. Teams are balanced by the House League committee after registration closes — there's nothing to prepare for and no one is cut.

  • Ages: 4U – 18U
  • How teams form: Balanced automatically after registration
  • Learn more: House League details →
Tryout-based

Rep & HP

Rep is competitive travel baseball for ages 9 and up; HP is our high-performance stream for players pursuing the next level. Both place players onto teams through tryouts each season — see the schedule below for your child's age group.

  • Ages: 9+ (Rep), select ages (HP)
  • How teams form: Tryout evaluation by coaching staff
  • Learn more: Rep details →  /  HP details →
Tryout day

What to bring

  • Glove & batTheir own equipment if they have it — loaners are available if not.
  • Cleats & comfortable clothesBaseball or turf cleats if available; running shoes work too.
  • Water bottleTryouts run for an hour or more, often outdoors.
  • Batting helmet, if ownedNot required — shared helmets are on hand for evaluations.
  • A positive attitudeCoaches are evaluating effort and coachability as much as skill.
  • Arrive 15 minutes earlyGives time to check in and warm up before things start.
